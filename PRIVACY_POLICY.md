# Privacy Policy for PicSome

**Last Updated: October 24, 2026**

**PicSome** ("we," "our," or "the app") is a photo browsing app for Android developed by theseuntaylor. It shows photos from [Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos/), lets you mark favourites, and lets you download photos to your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

---

## 1. Summary

- PicSome has **no accounts**, and we do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
- PicSome contains **no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools**.
- Everything the app saves stays **on your device**. We do not run any server of our own and do not receive any of your data.

---

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

- **Photo list and favourites**: The list of photos you have browsed (photo ID, photographer name, image size and links) and which photos you have marked as favourites are saved in a database inside the app, so the app works quickly and remembers your favourites.
- **Downloaded photos**: When you tap download, the photo is saved to your device's **Downloads** folder using Android's download manager. Downloaded photos are yours and stay on your device until you delete them.

This information never leaves your device, except as part of your own device backup (see section 5).

---

## 3. Information Shared with Third Parties

PicSome does not share, sell or transfer any personal information. To show photos, the app connects to the following services:

- **Lorem Picsum** (picsum.photos): The app requests the photo list and images from Picsum. Like any website, Picsum's servers receive standard network information such as your IP address and device type in order to send the images back. See the [Picsum website](https://picsum.photos/) for details.
- **Unsplash** (unsplash.com): Picsum's photos come from Unsplash. If you tap "View on unsplash.com", the photo's page opens in your web browser, and Unsplash's own [privacy policy](https://unsplash.com/privacy) applies there.

---

## 4. Permissions

- **Internet**: Needed to load photos.
- **Storage (Android 9 and older only)**: Needed to save downloaded photos to your Downloads folder. On Android 10 and newer, the app does not request this permission.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- Your favourites and the saved photo list remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, either of which deletes them.
- Downloaded photos remain in your Downloads folder until you delete them.
- If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved favourites in your backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account's settings.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

PicSome is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
